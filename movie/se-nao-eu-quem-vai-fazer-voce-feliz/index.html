Se Não Eu, Quem Vai Fazer Você Feliz?

PT

The plot is based on the book "Se Não Eu, Quem Vai Fazer Você Feliz? – Minha História de Amor com Chorão" (If Not Me, Who Will Make You Happy? – My Love Story with Chorão). The film explores the rise of Charlie Brown Jr. and Chorão’s journey in Santos—spanning the worlds of skateboarding and the stage—while focusing intimately on the intense, nearly 20-year romance between the musician and Graziela, revealing the man behind the idol and the creative process behind the band's greatest hits.
