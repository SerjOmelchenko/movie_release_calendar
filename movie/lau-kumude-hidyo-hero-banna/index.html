Lau Kumude Hidyo Hero Banna

EN

Lau Kumud Hidyo Hero Banna is an inspiring music video about dreams, friendship, and the journey of becoming a hero. Kumude leaves his home in Dang with big dreams in his heart and travels to Kathmandu to become a hero. On the way, he meets his two close friends who support him and help change his style with black sunglasses, black jeans, a tough jacket, and stylish black Dingo shoes. Looking smart and confident, Kumude walks proudly like a real hero. During the journey, he meets a kind tempo rider girl who supports and encourages him. Their adventure becomes difficult when the tempo tyre gets punctured on the road. Later, a helpful truck driver gives Kumude a ride towards Kathmandu. As they travel through hills, rivers, forests, and beautiful landscapes, Kumude enjoys the beauty of Nepal and learns that becoming a hero needs courage, confidence, friendship, and belief in dreams.

Starring Kumud Pant, Rabin Baraili, Shankar Raj Pahadi, Saraswati BK
