Heaven's Office Games

UK

Four carefree bachelors get into a car accident after a night of partying and find themselves in purgatory — a world of endless bureaucracy and chaotic digital reforms. At the Heaven’s Office, they receive their sentence: a direct express train to hell. Using their charm, they convince the purgatory secretary to give them a chance — seven days on Earth to atone for their sins. But once back, they realize they’ve fallen into a trap hidden in the perfectly ironic fine print of heaven.
