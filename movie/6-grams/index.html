6 grams

★ 8.0 / 10 · 2 votes CS

Four fathers meet in the secondary school gym, where the headmistress has summoned them after a bag containing a suspicious white powder was found amongst their sons’ belongings during a skiing trip. Each of them arrives with different excuses and the conviction that it is their child who is innocent. What’s more, the headmistress knows that a local councillor is trying to secure her job for his mistress, and she suspects that the whole situation is part of a campaign against her. The meeting thus quickly descends into a series of misunderstandings, accusations and increasingly absurd situations. One afternoon that spirals completely out of control for everyone involved.

Directed by Miroslav Ondruš Starring Ondřej Vetchý, David Novotný, Jiří Mádl, Martin Pechlát, Lenka Vlasáková
