In Transit

Late one night, rideshare driver Ayaan Malik accepts what appears to be an ordinary request. But when his mysterious passenger refuses to provide a destination, a routine ride descends into an unsettling journey through empty streets, repeating roads, and a reality that no longer makes sense. As the night unfolds, Ayaan begins to question everything around him—including the passenger in his back seat, the city outside his window, and whether some rides are meant to end at all. IN TRANSIT is a psychological thriller exploring isolation, choice, and the terrifying possibility that some paths cannot be escaped once they're taken.
