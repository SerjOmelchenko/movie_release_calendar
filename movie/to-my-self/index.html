To My Self

PL

A documentary impression of a spiritual journey inward. Iceland - a land of fissures, rugged formations, and places where fire meets ice - becomes not only the backdrop but a central character in the story. It is here that Marcel discovers a new sense of self, not by erasing his past, but by learning to live with it in harmony. Instead of rejection, there is acceptance; instead of struggle, a spiritual symbiosis in which the old and the new parts of his identity are able to coexist. Accompanying this process are breath, mindful presence, and simple daily rituals that lead him toward inner reconciliation.

Directed by Marta Kiebel Starring Marcel Krzak, Marta Kiebel, Sigurður Ingi Einarsson, Kamila Hetman, Einar Dór Guðmundsson
