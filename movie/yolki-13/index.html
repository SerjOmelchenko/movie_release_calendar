Yolki 13

RU

The stories of people's destinies, intertwined by genuine New Year's magic and sorcery in one grand story, are told through the serial number of the new installment, a symbol of change and transformation. The hero of one of the short stories, filming of which recently concluded in Moscow, is a wayward businessman named Igor, who finds himself caught in a whirlwind of adventures on New Year's Eve: a fortune teller places a curse on him that can only be lifted by reuniting with his daughter, whose existence Igor has long preferred to forget. He turns to the renowned and fashionable psychic Aleksandr Sheps for help. Furthermore, Igor must discover what truly matters most in life.

Directed by Zhora Kryzhovnikov, Olga Dolmatovskaya, Yury Korobeynikov Starring Sergey Burunov, Valentina Lyapina, Andrey Rozhkov, Olga Kartunkova, Valentina Mazunina
