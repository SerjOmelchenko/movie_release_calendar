Looking for Michael

ES

Looking for Michael tells the story of Sonia, a young woman diagnosed with Parkinson's disease. Instead of giving in to fear and sadness, she decides to live life differently. With the support of her music therapy group, whose members also live with the disease, and their teacher Pedro, Sonia sets out on a journey to meet Michael J. Fox, one of the best-known people living with Parkinson's.
