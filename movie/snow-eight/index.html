Snow Eight

RU

Mark, a 12-year-old sloppy teenager from a rich family, is sent by his mother from Moscow to Kamchatka to live with his harsh father, Kai, after another scandalous offense. Far from his usual life, the Internet and school friends, the boy finds himself in the world of dog sled racing. Working with animals — the snow eight charming husky dogs — becomes a real challenge for him. Gradually, Mark finds friends — Anya and Petka — and discovers the power of the team, where trust and support are important. And mom and dad have to change their views on raising a child and, most importantly, look at each other with completely new eyes.

Directed by Vitaly Dudka Starring Nikolay Naumov, Ida Galich, Eva Smirnova, Misha Schults
