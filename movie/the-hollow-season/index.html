In the remote mountain town of Graven Hollow, autumn is more than just a season — it’s a warning. When a group of college friends arrives for the annual fall festival, they’re drawn into a chilling legend about the Hollow Man — a masked figure born of fire and betrayal, who returns to collect a blood debt.
