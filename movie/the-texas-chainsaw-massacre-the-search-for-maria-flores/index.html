The Texas Chainsaw Massacre: The Search for Maria Flores

EN

Fanfilm prequel of the first Texas Chain Saw Massacre, based on the lore of the game by Gun Media. In April 1973, a college student named Maria Flores goes missing in rural Texas during a photography trip to capture the blooming spring wildflowers. Maria's younger sister, Ana Flores, refuses to give up on her. She gathers a group of college friends to head out to the last known area Maria visited.
