Solo Vivo

A profound narrative about the history, resistance, and social impact of the Ecological Farmers Market (FAE) of Porto Alegre, RS. A sensitive look at the dignity of rural work, resilience in the face of climate extremes, and the strength of a community united by respect for the land to nurture human relationships and promote health through healthy eating.
