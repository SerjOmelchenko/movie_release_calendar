Show me From Behind the Wall

PT

After several years of distance, Mary Jane agrees to reunite with a group of former friends for a trip meant to offer reconnection and rest. However, the reunion quickly becomes uncomfortable. Old wounds, unresolved conflicts, and buried secrets begin to surface, turning the experience into an increasingly tense and claustrophobic one. Isolated in a house far from the city, the women are forced to confront the past and face the emotional consequences of deep trauma. The film offers a disturbing portrait of the psychological deterioration of a woman pushed to her limits, where sanity dissolves and nothing is exactly as it seems.
