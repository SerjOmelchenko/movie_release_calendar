Distraught with emotion after letting Mr. Knoflick destroy half the universe, Economy-Man sits at a crossroads, knowing he must stop Knoflick before the entire economy is destroyed but searching for a team who can help him beat this seemingly unassailable foe. Meanwhile, three other lost heroes lives intersect with his: Loggo, searching for his best friend, who thinks he is dead; Fry-Man, grieving after giving up the mask after the tragic death of his girlfriend, Gwel; and Bob the Soap, a once proud warrior who finds himself lonelier than ever. The four heroes, shrouded with loneliness, will band together to stop Knoflick from destroying the economy, but can they defeat him and his army before it's too late?
